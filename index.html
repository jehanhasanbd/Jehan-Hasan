AdaptiveBeliefMem: Memory-Augmented LLM Research

Research • 2026

Jehan Hasan

Memory framework with implicit belief state and uncertainty-gated explicit memory mechanism.

Autonomous AI agents powered by Large Language Models (LLMs) increasingly depend on long-horizon memory to execute multi-step tasks and support complex reasoning across extended interaction trajectories. Existing approaches rely predominantly on static summarization or fixed-schedule retrieval policies, neither of which adapts to the informational demands of the task at hand, leaving systems vulnerable to the loss of critical details and the propagation of hallucinated information across future interactions.
To address these limitations, we propose AdaptiveBeliefMem, an agentic memory management framework that couples an implicit belief state with an uncertainty-gated explicit decision trigger, mediated by a frozen downstream reasoner for inference-time decoupling. A trainable builder encodes the full conversational trajectory into a fixed-capacity latent belief vector via a GRU-style gated update, ensuring memory footprint remains bounded regardless of trajectory length. A lightweight uncertainty estimator selectively triggers the externalization of a structured decision trace only when the implicit belief state is likely insufficient for reliable reasoning. Hard Top-K selection with Straight-Through Estimation (STE) enforces gradient sparsity during training, and the frozen reasoner operates exclusively on externalized traces, remaining fully decoupled from the trainable memory components.
Evaluated on the LoCoMo long-horizon conversational benchmark against seven baselines, AdaptiveBeliefMem achieves state-of-the-art performance on multi-hop reasoning (F1: 52.2) and open domain question answering (F1: 28.8), demonstrating that uncertainty-gated selective externalization is a more efficient and effective strategy for cross-session evidence integration than full context injection or fixed-schedule retrieval.

AgenticNutrition: Personalized Nutrition Management AI Agent

Research • 2026

Jehan Hasan

Context-aware AI agent for personalized nutrition using LangGraph and LangChain.

In response to the global rise in chronic disease and lifestyle-related issues, personalized nutrition has emerged as a key strategy for promoting healthier eating habits and dis- ease prevention. Traditional nutrition management systems often fail to address disease-specific dietary guidelines, personalization, and the complex structure of multiple disease diagnoses. The response may contain seasonally available, lower-cost food, ignoring the weather and place. This paper proposes a novel nutrition management AI agent using Large Language Models (LLMs) which uses MISTRAL-7b, and achieved Constraint Ad-herence Rate (CAR) 0.8, recall 0.8834, implemented as an intellectual multi-agent frame- work that leverages users’ medical data to deliver personalized, geographically available, and cost-efficient, disease-specific nutrition management guidelines.

GANNDDI: Graph Attention Neural Network based Drug-Drug Interaction

Research • 2026

Jehan Hasan

Intelligent Prediction of Potential Drug–Drug Interactions.

When multiple drugs are taken together, they can sometimes cause unexpected and harmful interactions that reduce treatment effectiveness or even endanger patients’ health. As more people today take several medicines at once, it has become very important to develop computer-based methods that can identify these risks early. Recent advances in artificial intelligence (AI) and deep learning have shown strong potential for predicting DDIs. However, many existing models still overlook how the small structural parts of drug molecules interact with one another. Ignoring these detailed molecular relationships can make the predictions less accurate and less reliable for real-world use in healthcare and drug discovery.
